Who We Are

A Kenya-based destination management company

Multiscope Travel is a trusted corporate travel and destination management company based in Nairobi, Kenya. We create authentic Kenyan travel experiences that connect people to culture, landscapes, wildlife, and warm hospitality.

As a full-service travel partner, we provide MICE planning, transport and logistics, travel documentation support, flight coordination, and tailor-made safaris for corporate teams, delegations, groups, and individual travellers.

Our work is grounded in practical local knowledge, professional coordination, and a commitment to experiences that are seamless, responsible, and memorable.
