Who We Are

A boutique professional firm for managed travel and MICE logistics

Multiscope Travel is a trusted corporate travel management and destination management company based in Nairobi, Kenya. We help organizations plan, control, and support travel across Kenya with the responsiveness of a boutique firm and the discipline expected from a professional TMC.

As a full-service travel partner, we provide managed business travel, MICE logistics, transport and movement control, travel documentation support, flight coordination, duty of care workflows, analytics, and incentive experiences for corporate teams, NGOs, delegations, executives, and specialist groups.

Our work is grounded in practical local knowledge, supplier accountability, traveller support, reporting discipline, and a commitment to programs that are safe, efficient, responsible, and commercially clear.
