Who We Are

Come Travel Kenya is a trusted Destination Management Company (DMC) based in Nairobi, Kenya, established in 2019. We specialize in creating authentic Kenyan travel experiences that connect people to the country's rich culture, breathtaking landscapes, and warm hospitality.

As a full-service DMC, we provide complete travel management solutions — from MICE (Meetings, Incentives, Conferences, and Exhibitions) and transport & logistics, to travel documentation and tailor-made safaris. Our experiences feature cultural, historical, prehistoric, and mountain-climbing adventures, showcasing the best of Kenya's diversity and beauty.

We also manage air travel logistics, including air ticketing and travel insurance, ensuring that every journey with us is seamless, safe, and memorable.

At Come Travel Kenya, we believe travel is more than movement — it's about meaningful connections, responsible tourism, and celebrating the true spirit of Kenya.
