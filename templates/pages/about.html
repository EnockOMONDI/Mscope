Who We Are

A Kenya-based Destination Management Company

Come Travel Kenya is a trusted Destination Management Company based in Nairobi, Kenya, established in 2019. We specialize in creating authentic Kenyan travel experiences that connect people to the country’s rich culture, breathtaking landscapes, and warm hospitality.

As a full-service DMC, we provide complete travel management solutions — from MICE meetings, incentives, conferences, exhibitions, transport and logistics, to travel documentation and tailor-made safaris.

We also manage air travel logistics, including air ticketing and travel insurance, ensuring that every journey with us is seamless, safe, and memorable.
