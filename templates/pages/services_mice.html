Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers on scope, geography, timelines, and how we support corporate buyers and travel partners.

What types of events do you handle?
We handle corporate meetings, incentive programs, conferences, exhibitions, product launches, retreats, delegation travel, and hybrid programs that combine business sessions with Kenyan destination experiences.
How far in advance should we book?
For conferences and larger delegate movements, earlier planning gives better venue availability and supplier rates. Smaller meetings can often be coordinated faster once the brief, dates, and estimated numbers are clear.
Do you work outside Nairobi?
Yes. We coordinate MICE programs across Kenya, including Nairobi, the Coast, conservancies, safari circuits, lakeside regions, and other destination venues depending on your objectives.
What is included in your packages?
Packages are tailored, but typically include venue sourcing, supplier coordination, transport, accommodation support, delegate logistics, event production, activity planning, and on-ground management.
Can you handle international delegates?
Yes. We can support airport arrivals, local transport, accommodation coordination, domestic flights, travel documentation guidance, and hosted experiences for international delegates.
How quickly can you turn around a proposal?
Turnaround depends on event complexity. Once we have dates, group size, budget range, preferred locations, and service scope, we can prepare a focused proposal with practical next steps.
